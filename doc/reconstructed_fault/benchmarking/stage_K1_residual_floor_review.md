# K1 nonlinear residual-floor recovery

## Bounded review of existing infrastructure

The accepted linear-solver correction is the starting point. No adaptive-
forcing integration or Picard/Newton blending is added for K1.

| Mechanism | Conventional ASPECT path | Reconstructed-fault coupled path |
|---|---|---|
| Eisenstat–Walker | `do_one_defect_correction_Stokes_step` calls `compute_Eisenstat_Walker_linear_tolerance`; the helper implements choices 1/2 and its safeguards. | The reconstructed branch returns before that loop. The condensed solve reads the requested Stokes tolerance at each invocation and uses the same request for FGMRES and fresh verification. No EW update is inherited. |
| Newton derivative scaling | The Newton scheme ramps viscosity derivatives using the residual or switches Picard/Newton; the standard assembler uses the derivative factor. | The factor is deliberately zero: frozen Maxwell coefficients give an affine bulk law, while the nonlinear surface derivative is explicitly in B/G/K_V. Ramping those blocks would change the coupled Jacobian, not reuse the viscosity mechanism. |
| Residual assembly | Newton cell/QP assemblers fill -R; velocity and scaled-continuity norms are combined. `compute_initial_newton_residual` temporarily zeros velocity for its reference. | Reuses the assemblers and vector/constraint infrastructure. Its non-committing evaluator also includes frozen Maxwell/fault loads; its zero-velocity reference reproduces the mathematical convention without calling the helper's constraint-mutating lifecycle. |
| Stopping/globalization | Conventional defect correction has a large-reduction skip and its own line search, which can return the last candidate on exhaustion. | Independent bulk/free-surface criteria, fixed merit scales and strict Armijo exhaustion are retained; inheriting that exhaustion behavior would violate the approved rollback contract. |
| Linear solve / lifecycle | Block AMG, pressure scaling, physical normalization, failure signaling and checkpoint/postprocessor infrastructure. | Reused, with nonsymmetric FGMRES condensation, verified pressure complement, fresh residuals, homogeneous directions and private atomic publication. |

(`compute_initial_newton_residual`, `perform_line_search`, EW helper),

## Mixed criterion under verification

Let A0 be the first constrained bulk matrix and x0 the owned first physical
iterate with pressure divided by ASPECT's pressure scaling. Compute once

    d_i = sum_c sum_(j in c) |A0_ij| max_(j in c) |x0_j|,
    rho_b = epsilon_machine * sqrt(sum_owned_rows d_i^2).

The sum is MPI-reduced once. This is a blockwise absolute-row-sum bound for
A0 acting on a machine-precision bulk perturbation at the initial magnitudes;
there is no fitted multiplier or observation of the stalled residual. It is
not a general condition/error estimator. It does not claim to bound arbitrary
subsequent iterates or physical discretization error.

The authorized mixed criterion is bulk_norm < nonlinear_tolerance*s_b + rho_b.
Use s_b + rho_b/nonlinear_tolerance as the **fixed** bulk scale in both
convergence and Armijo merit. The independent surface criterion is unchanged.
The pressure-compatibility cap continues to use the original relative bulk
target, not the new allowance. Linear requests, fresh checks and total linear
budget are untouched. No history is published before both criteria pass.

The specification/current design now state this acceptance change explicitly.
The local gradient correction remains useful: it avoids spending the precision
budget on removable cancellation. Verification also reports the computed
velocity correction at the convergence check, to compare attainable physical
changes against the independently specified K1 error budget.

Final diagnostic outcome, correction and gate results will be recorded here
before completion. K1 remains unmet and K2 remains pending.
