## Implement three separately reviewable changes

**1. Separate the mature trace from constitutive state.** Follow `MaterialFaultTrace::Label`, `initialize`, `accept_section_rates`, `FaultStokesCoupling::BulkPoint::add_balance`, and `PhaseFieldFault`'s accepted/candidate sections and checkpoint reader. Remove concrete RSF types, mandatory age arguments and age evolution from geometry and geometric transfer. Put age, initial shear and Maxwell memory under their physical owners. Pass the material response needed by the balance at the same sampled location and time level. Preserve the current interpolation of accepted vertex age before evaluating nonlinear friction, the speed convention, pressure sampling, and the projected stress returned to Stokes. Keep trace/profile lifetimes sound when an accepted or candidate object moves. Migrate diagnostics, assemblers and tests that read combined label records. A composed read-only view for RSF output is appropriate. It must not become a second writable history.

**2. Separate the growing strip law from transfer and direction scoring.** Follow `SectionHistory`, `SectionTransfer`, `solve_section`, `candidate_onset`, and `MidpointSystem::assemble_stage`. Geometric transfer and the orientation score must work without importing an RSF type or accepting an age. Let the material supply the candidate resistance and correct derivative through a narrow operation. Preserve the robust signed scalar solver and its low-speed arithmetic in the RSF implementation. The growing law uses age at half-duration for friction and full-duration for publication, including aging of unformed strips. Preserve the distinct mature time discretization. Move law-specific admissibility checks, including the RSF timestep restriction, behind the same boundary so a state-free law is never tested against RSF parameters.

Every proposed angle must still obtain its own resistance using the complete strip and its candidate arclength. Only the direct orientation score uses the disk. Keep the condensed cross-cell and cross-rank tangent, the small traction remainder that recovers native shear lost to cancellation in cold creep, and the independent check on the rate recoverable from stored point increments. Keep friction history allocation proportional to the state the selected law actually declares. A sentinel age, setting the RSF evolution coefficient to zero, or default-initializing an unused age array does not demonstrate that the dependency was removed.

**3. Return coordination to the simulator.** Trace the literal `"growing fault"` check in parameter parsing, the concrete material cast in the nonlinear scheme, `Material::solve_midpoint_fields`, and `MidpointSystem`'s use of the material to reach simulator services. Dispatch according to the selected midpoint field and the actual constitutive operations available. Validate compatibility once the configuration and provider are known. An unsupported material must fail with a clear diagnostic before the phase could be silently skipped. Let the simulator-side coupling call the global solver and access FE/MPI services directly. The material supplies physical parameters, local responses and constitutive history. The midpoint algorithm may continue to require the qualified Maxwell–AT1 response. Document the operations it actually needs. Existing material save/load and simulator signals may delegate to the appropriate owner.

Use the smallest existing extension point or a narrow typed interface. A renamed method that still hands the entire solve to a concrete material leaves the dependency in place. Preserve temperature and companion-field dispatch, completion signaling before acceptance, rollback, cutback and pre-solve access to restored state. Keep existing parameter spellings/defaults, output meanings and feature-off behavior. Retain separate mature and growing global algorithms. Match header/source locations, document new public contracts, and avoid changes to the upstream material base or simulator core unless the integration actually requires them.

## Prove the separation

For a growing strip with V = Vfree − M f, the independent result is V = Vfree/(1+Mκ), f = κV and condensed slope κ/(1+Mκ). Use distinct nonzero rates and measures, including reversal on the signed path. Exercise actual transfer, candidate-angle evaluation, returned tangent and acceptance. Show the mature geometry/transfer can also use the law under its existing speed convention. Check that this test consumes no age input, allocates no age per label or strip and emits no age history in its checkpoint. Keep unrelated bulk and kinematic state.
