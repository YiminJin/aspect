Use up to four MPI ranks total on the laptop, with one thread per rank. Run the two sizes sequentially.

Check the branch's growing-strip formulation, initial tip position, phase resolution, 70 MPa friction pressure, constant RSF coefficients, preloading clock and the change to steady drive at 0.025 s. Preserve the uniform slip/state policy on the branch actually being tested. Check the growing midpoint admissibility formula on that branch. At the published revision its full-step limit is 7.698 microseconds. Preserve the supplied 7.5 microsecond cap unless the actual implementation and a documented verification justify a different experiment.

Separate initialization and native CSV output costs from representative solve time. Report accepted and rejected attempts, nonlinear iterations, wall time per accepted interval, peak rank-zero memory and output bytes. ## 3. Distinguish slip, damage advance, and a physical event

Use only accepted clocks for the history. During the preload, inventory damage and formed sections outside the initial support. Count evidence for spontaneous recurrence only after the drive is steady. Plot maximum slip rate, slip rate at the last previously formed station immediately behind the evolving tip, and connected tip position against the same time axis. Use the formation flag alongside at least two connected phase contours, for example 0.01 and 0.05 relative to their initial positions. If the data permit, show a space–time picture of signed station rate and overlay the connected tip. Compare extension to cell and station spacing. A one-station jump, a peak far behind the tip, and several contours moving across initially intact rock have different interpretations.
